    ExpenseTracker User Guide

    Quick tour to mastering your financial ledger
    🚀
    1. Dashboard & Cash Flow Matrix
    The top 4 metric cards summarize your overall financial health:
    • Total Inflow: All incoming salary, freelance payouts, and investment returns.
    • Total Outflow: All expenses and recurring subscription charges.
    • Net Cash Flow: Your real-time surplus or deficit (Inflow minus Outflow). Positive is colored green, negative is red.
    • Savings Rate: The percentage of your inflow preserved rather than spent.
    💸
    2. Inflows, Expenses & Subscriptions
    Log transactions individually or mark them recurring:
    • Add Expense: Click "+ Add Expense" or press Ctrl+K. Select category, amount, and date.
    • Add Income: Click "+ Add Income" to record salary, bonuses, or gifts.
    • Recurring Subscriptions: Check "Recurring" on any expense to manage it automatically under Subscriptions.
    • Switch Streams: Click "Expenses" or "Incomes" toggle tabs above the main transaction table.
    📷
    3. Smart Receipt & Bill OCR
    Save time typing manual bills with on-device Optical Character Recognition:
    • Click "+ Add Expense" and look at the Smart Receipt Scanner box at the top.
    • Drop an image or tap "Scan Receipt / Bill" to choose a photo or use your camera.
    • Tesseract.js runs 100% locally in your browser. Your financial photos are never uploaded to any third-party server.
    • The amount, date, store merchant, and suggested category will automatically populate into the form!
    🎯
    4. Envelopes & Savings Goals
    Plan your future and stop lifestyle creep:
    • Budgets: Set monthly spending caps per category (Food, Utilities, Entertainment). Visual progress bars warn you when exceeding 80% or 100%.
    • Savings Goals: Create target funds (Emergency Fund, Vacation, New Car) and deposit money with a single click.
    📊
    5. Executive Reports & Excel Export
    Get institutional-grade financial statements:
    • Executive Report (Excel): Multi-sheet workbook with AutoFilter, Freeze Panes, and live =SUM formulas.
    • Statement (PDF): Clean, audit-ready financial statement with net flows and category breakdowns.
    • Import Spreadsheets: Re-import your Excel or CSV ledgers anytime to restore backups.
    ⚡
    6. Power Shortcuts
    Navigate effortlessly with keyboard hotkeys:
    • Ctrl + K or Cmd + K: Open Spotlight Command Palette to trigger any action instantly.
    • Alt + C: Toggle Floating Quick Calculator & Bill Splitter.
    • /: Instantly jump to and focus table search.
    • Esc: Dismiss any active dialog, palette, or menu.
    🧮
    7. Quick Calculator & Bill Splitter
    Do fast on-screen math without leaving your active workflow:
    • Draggable Floating Bubble: Click or drag the gold calculator bubble at the bottom right. Press Alt + C anytime to toggle it.
    • Bill Splitter: Tap 1x, ÷2, ÷3, ÷4, ÷5 to divide group dinners or shared bills evenly.
    • 1-Tap Insert: Click "Use Amount" to auto-fill the calculated total directly into your active Expense or Income form!
    • Physical Keyboard: Supports full numpad entry, Enter (=), Backspace, and Esc to minimize.
